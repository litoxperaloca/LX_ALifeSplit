description=[h1]LX A-Life Split-Screen Fix[/h1]
description=[i]Project A-Life NPCs that see all of you[/i]
description=A-Life is built around one player. In split-screen its NPCs only ever noticed player 1. Now they notice everyone.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]NPCs perceive every local player[/b] - A-Life asks the game for “the players” through one locator used by [i]all[/i] of its AI - perception, spawning and despawning, decision pacing, shelter…
description=[*][b]Encounters, raids and world events reach everyone[/b] - Encounters could only revolve around player 1 and were discarded when the chosen player was someone else. Road encounters while driving watched one player.
description=[*][b]Player 2's emotes are player 2's[/b] - Surrendering, waving, asking NPCs to stand down, insulting - everything you “say” through gestures was delivered as if player 1 did it.
description=[*][b]The talk window opens where you are[/b] - With two or more players the conversation window opens in the speaking player's half of the screen, and falls back to the classic layout when the cinematic…
description=[*][b]Reputation tab no longer traps the controller[/b] - The Reputation tab in the character info screen did not respond to a gamepad and left it stuck. LB/RB now change tabs and B closes, like every vanilla tab.
description=[*][b]NPC aim resets between targets[/b] - An NPC's aim ramp kept its value when it switched from player 1 to player 2, because both shared one key. Each player now gets their own.
description=[/list]
